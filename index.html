Welcome to Treo

Treo is a simple orientation aid for your phone. When things feel confusing, one big button shows where you are, what is here, and what is likely next.

Open the app to try it now, or share your information to sign up as a tester.

Open Treo Share your information

This is an early test version. It is not a safety or medical device, so please don't rely on it for anything urgent.

Try it in three steps
  1. Tap Open Treo.
  2. Tap My Places, then Use My Location. Give the place a name and tap Save Place.
  3. Go back and tap I Need Help. Treo should tell you where you are.
Put it on your home screen

Android (Chrome): tap Open Treo, then the menu (three dots), then Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone (Safari): tap Open Treo, then the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

What stays on your phone

Your places, contacts and events are saved on your own phone, not on our servers.

To find an address and draw the map, Treo asks OpenStreetMap, so the address you type is sent there. Please test with places you are comfortable with.
